AN ANIMATED SPORTS SHORT — UPRIGHT, ABOUT 63 SECONDS, 12 SHOTS.

Everything in this film is invented: the clubs, the players, the stadium, the
channel and the advertising boards. It is not a recreation of any real match,
broadcast or person. It is animation in the Papermorph style — painted sets and
jointed paper puppets on a stage — directed as if it were a televised broadcast.

THE WORLD
Home side: Kuhsar Tehran. Red shirts with white sleeves, chest sponsor
"Payam Kala", sleeve sponsor "Nilgoon". Their keeper wears green.
Away side: Darya Ahvaz. All blue with white collars.
Stadium: Setareh Stadium, at night under floodlights. Striped green turf,
LED boards around the pitch carrying "Payam Kala", "Sepidar", "Nilgoon",
"Avangar", "Mahoot", "Zarrin" and "Kusha". The upper tier is packed with
red-shirted home fans; the lower rows beside the running track are empty.
Channel: GolNama, watermark "GolNama | golnama.tv" top-left on a slanted black
corner bar. Programme name for the replay transition: "Tonight's Football".
Scoreboard: top-right, "KUHSAR 0 - 0 DARYA" with a running clock.
Referee in fluorescent yellow.

Darya Ahvaz line-up, 3-5-2:
  Keeper 1 Farzin Golshan
  Defenders 76 Arya Bakhtiar, 23 Babak Rostami, 66 Subotic
  Midfield 99 Reza Nobakht, 11 Behrooz Kian, 50 Lucas Bernardo,
           37 Saman Arvin, 6 Mahmoud Dehghan
  Forwards 14 Kaveh Mehri, 9 Javad Parsa
  Head coach Mansour Davari, white shirt, black scarf
Kuhsar players seen: 3 Farshid Kaviani, 6 Hossein Niknam, 7 Soroush Aram,
  33 and 5, the full-back Daniyal Rastegar, and the green-shirted keeper.

TONE
Electric, dramatic, surprising, tense. The contrast between the home crowd's
shock and the away side's joy carries the whole piece.

ONE LINE
In the 4th minute Babak Rostami punishes a lapse in the Kuhsar back line and
scores the opening goal for Darya Ahvaz, and the stadium falls into stunned
silence.

SHOT 1 — 00:00 to 00:06
Long master shot, high angle from the upper tier. The camera pans right with
the counter-attack, pushes in slightly as the move enters the box, then drifts
back left after the goal. Rostami, 23 in blue, breaks from the left flank past
the Kuhsar defenders and into the penalty area. At 00:03, one on one, he slides
the ball low past the green-shirted keeper. At 00:04 it reaches the net and he
runs away to the bottom right of frame, while the red defenders stand and stare.
Commentary: the commentator is mid-sentence about a comfortable start when his
voice lifts suddenly — the chance forming for Darya, the lapse in the Kuhsar
back line, and an early goal.
Sound: excited commentary over stadium ambience; the hum turns to shock, with a
small pocket of away fans cheering. No music.
On screen: scoreboard top-right, clock running 03:07 to 03:13, the score
flipping to "KUHSAR 0 - 1 DARYA" at 00:06. GolNama watermark top-left.
Cut.

SHOT 2 — 00:07 to 00:13

Commentary: the Kuhsar line-up had not even been introduced yet when Rastegar's

SHOT 12 — 00:53 to 01:03

Commentary: the most important thing so far tonight is exactly this — the lapse
in the back line, and Kuhsar's goal breached this early.
On screen: the scoreboard is gone in this shot; only the GolNama watermark and
the black corner bar remain.
Final cut. End of film.

NOTES FOR THE DIRECTOR
Key beat: 00:03 to 00:04, the inside-foot finish past the keeper. Everything
else exists to set it up or to turn it over.
Emotional contrast, 00:14 to 00:28: cut between the stunned, head-down defender
(shots 3 and 8), the small away end in full voice (shots 4 and 7) and the cold,
motionless coach (shot 6).
Structure: a hook with no preamble, the goal inside four seconds (00:00-00:06);
then the reaction section showing all four sides of it while the line-up graphic
runs (00:07-00:31); then the analytical multi-angle replay after the programme
wipe (00:32-01:03), the goal taken apart from four different angles.
